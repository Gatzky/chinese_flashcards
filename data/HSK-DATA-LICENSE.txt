Example sentences are adapted from individual Tatoeba contributors. Each
sentenceSource field identifies the contributor, sentence, source URL, and
license. Tatoeba sentence data is licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

Character etymology notes are adapted from the dictionary data in Make Me a
Hanzi by Skishore:
https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/tree/bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92
The dictionary data is derived from Unihan and CJKlib and is distributed under
LGPL-3.0-or-later, subject to the notices in
data/MAKE-ME-A-HANZI-LICENSE.txt. Per-card etymologySource fields identify the
source.
Etymology notes are included only when present in the source dictionary; no
etymology is supplied for entries without source data.
The source dictionary has no etymology data for these single-character
entries: 七, 万, 为, 久, 也, 买, 信, 六, 关, 写, 十, 千, 去, 向, 夏, 它, 岁,
更, 条, 用, 的, 着, 这, 那.
